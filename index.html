Grade 10 Subject Choice Guide

This free guide helps Grade 10 students choose their three O/L Basket Subjects wisely. Pick a dream career or a subject combination and enter your expected grades. The guide then shows the A/L Streams you can enter, the university and other higher education courses open to you, and matching careers, based on the UGC Student Handbook 2025/2026.
